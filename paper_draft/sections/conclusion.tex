\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

\para{Summary.} We asked where instruction tuning changes a base model's next-token distribution in ways a learned predictor cannot anticipate from simple base-side context, and what kinds of tokens those are. The answer is sharp. Per-token base$\to$instruct divergence is mostly a predictable function of \emph{base uncertainty}: a \gbt\ trained only on base-side features recovers $R^2 \approx 0.60$--$0.68$ across all three \qwen\ pairs (Spearman $\rho$ up to $0.89$), with \texttt{base\_tok\_logprob} and \texttt{base\_entropy} carrying nearly all the signal and position barely mattering. But the leftover residual is \emph{not} noise. It is structured and alignment-specific: across all three scales it concentrates significantly on safety prompts (\justeval's safety-tagged subset shows positive mean residual with rank-biserial up to $0.322$, Mann--Whitney $p \to 0$) and on discourse/structural pivots and alignment-injected content---refusal-recovery turns (\eg ``If you have any other questions\ldots''), persona and identity tokens (``Alibaba''), and knowledge-cutoff framing (``October'')---rather than the generic stylistic tokens that dominate the \emph{predictable} part of the shift.

\para{Takeaway.} Most of what alignment does to the token distribution is anticipated by how uncertain the base model already was; what it does \emph{beyond} that is a small, readable signature of safety, discourse control, and injected identity.

\para{Future work.} Several directions follow directly. (1) Score base-decoded or held-out reference sequences in addition to instruct-decoded ones, removing the which-model-generated-the-text confound from the residual. (2) Add an SFT-only model pair to separate the contributions of supervised fine-tuning from preference optimization. (3) Train a residual probe on base hidden states and attribute it with logit-lens tooling \citep{logitlens2025} to localize \emph{where} in the network the unpredictable shift originates. (4) Use the residual itself as an unsupervised detector of alignment-driven behavior and validate it against held-out safety and persona labels.
